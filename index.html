Resume

School

I joined the Gaming and Multimedia program at Questar III, where I learned to code and was introduced to the basics of music theory, which pushed me to go deeper on my own. Even though it can be complicated, I really enjoy the process.

Me

My name is Judah Bertos, and my channel name is Xen0 Music. I am passionate about music, especially collecting CDs and vinyl. I am self-taught in music theory, and I’ve released one full album with twenty songs, as well as a single. I enjoy blending multiple genres because it makes my music more interesting, but I tend to prefer synthetic sounds.

Experiences

I’ve worked on many projects as a lead audio designer, playtester, and level designer. Two examples include Bready or Knot, a 2D bread-themed platformer that needed abstract, synthetic music and chiptune sounds that I sourced myself, and Harpoon Bird Fishing, a game about a fictional sport involving a harpoon gun. That project required more acoustic music, pushing me out of my comfort zone, and I'm really proud of how it turned out.

My Future

Looking ahead, I want to work in audio design and music production for games. I also plan to continue making my own music on the side. I enjoy being part of this field, and I hope to keep growing, developing my skills, and sharing my work with others.
